Tokens per dollar

Most speed. Per dollar.

For each board with a listed price, we take its fastest sourced generation speed on a common model and divide by the board's listed price. Higher is better.

How this is computed tok/s per $100 = best measured gen tok/s ÷ listed USD price × 100. Both inputs are sourced (links in every row); the ratio is simple arithmetic on them, not an estimate. Prices are the listed prices in our board price list and change often. Power supply, storage, cooling and shipping are not included. Different sources use different settings, so treat close values as a tie.
