How to choose.

  • Small models (1–2B) are fine on a Pi 5. Its best sourced run on Qwen2.5 1.5B is 11.73 tok/s (source ↗), above the 10 tok/s mark we treat as comfortable chat speed. An RK3588 board reached 16.69 tok/s on the same model using its NPU (source ↗), but needs RKLLM-converted models.
  • 3B models are where the Jetson pulls away. On Llama 3.2 3B the Orin Nano Super's best is 43.1 tok/s (source ↗) against 5.95 on a Pi 5 (source ↗) and 2.65 on the AI HAT+ 2 (source ↗).
  • 8B models need a GPU or a lot of patience. Llama 3.1 8B: 19.1 tok/s on the Orin Nano Super (source ↗), 3.72 on RK3588 (source ↗), 2.05 on a Pi 5 (source ↗).
  • Count the extras. A Pi 5 needs a power supply, a cooler and a microSD card; the Jetson includes its power supply. Each board page has a "What you need to get running" list, and the tokens-per-dollar ranking shows which board gives the most speed per dollar.
