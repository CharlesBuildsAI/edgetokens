Methodology

How we check.

  • We only publish numbers someone actually measured and published: vendor benchmark pages, papers, reviews, GitHub issues and community posts. Every row links to its source.
  • We don't estimate or extrapolate. If a source gives a range or a "~", we record what it says and keep its wording in the notes.
  • Dates: "published" means the source's publish date, "commit" a git commit date, "tested" the test date in the post, "accessed" when no date was shown (the date we read it).
  • Source labels are provenance, not verification. Vendor reported, academic paper, independent publication and community reported describe the publisher recorded in the dataset. EdgeTokens transcribes these reports and has not independently reproduced the runs.
  • Fastest is not typical. Headline speeds and buyer rankings use the highest single reported result in the relevant group. A range spans different setups, not repeated trials under one protocol; no typical speed is inferred.
  • Comparisons are approximate. Prompt length, context, runtime version, power mode and cooling differ between sources.
  • Tokens per dollar: the value ranking and board picker divide a sourced speed by a sourced listed price. That is arithmetic on two sourced numbers, and every row links to both sources. Prices exclude PSU, storage, cooling and shipping.
  • Affiliate links: some buy links may earn us a commission. They never change the numbers.
